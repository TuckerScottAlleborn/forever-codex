The patch log

Every dated change the codex draws on, from the reveal to the first raid tier. A book that predates an entry here says so in its footer.

  1. Sep 12
    Forever revealed

    A permanent level-60 branch of WoW, with the Skyborne and the direction for updated racials.

    the lorethe races

  2. Sep 17
    The public beta opens

    It runs to October 21.

  3. Sep 22
    Race and class combinations

    High Order and Windshaper Skyborne split by faction; Human Hunter, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman highlighted for the Alliance.

    the five races

  4. Sep 30
    The Priest deep dive

    Fear Ward and Devouring Plague become baseline for every Priest. Gnome’s Confounding Flash and Contingency Plan, Human Divine Grace and Dwarf Chastise are introduced, and Night Elf Starshards and Elune’s Grace change. Divine Grace and Contingency Plan have since gone from 5- to 10-minute cooldowns.

    the priest sheetpriest race leans

  5. Oct 8
    Another racial pass signaled

    Lead developer commentary after community concern about strong active racials and weapon-skill disparities.

  6. Oct 16
    The last beta build with class changes

    Also the first major racial pass.

    classes

    • Elemental: Lava Burst moves up to 21 points and Elemental Mastery returns at 31
    • Arcane Blast redesigned around stacking spell power, its raw damage cut 40%; Ice Lance against frozen targets cut from +300% to +200%
    • Champion of the Light cut from 20/40/60% to 10/20/30%; Righteous Fury’s Holy threat raised from 60% to 90%
    • Hunter pets: the Crocolisk’s healing reduction cut from 50% to 25%, the Bat’s casting slow halved, the Bird of Prey toned down; pets now inherit the player’s higher crit
    • Hunters can switch automatically between Auto Attack and Auto Shot, and keep one Fire and one Ice trap active at once
    • Shadow talents revised; Discipline’s Power in Light refined and Mental Strength moved earlier
    • Grace of Air can run alongside Windfury or Flametongue
    • Furor keeps Cat energy through a quick shift into Bear

    races

    • Human Sword Specialization becomes Ruthless, +2% crit
    • Dwarf’s beta Mace Specialization becomes Earth Fury; Find Treasure gains bonus chest loot
    • Wind-Blessed 1% → 3%; Walk on Air to a 90-second cooldown; Read Leyline usable while moving, with more Ley Lines
    • Eureka! becomes an 8-second window with no charges, and supports Power Word: Shield
    • Quickness’s movement speed now stacks

    the classesracial history

  7. Oct 21
    The public beta closes

    Its last day doubles as a public stress test. No new class pass.

  8. Oct 26
    The final pre-launch racial pass
    • Ruthless 2% → 1%; The Human Spirit 5% → 10%
    • Earth Fury removed; Artisan’s Precision, +1% hit, added
    • Wind-Blessed 3% → 2%; Read Leyline redesigned around 15 seconds of +50% regeneration, and an hour-long Attuned state at +100%
    • Night Elf and Gnome untouched

    Blizzard framed it as racial identity without racials so strong they override player preference, and kept weapon specializations out.

    the races book

  9. Nov 4
    Launch

    At 3:00 p.m. PST. Early Year 1, with new ground in Hyjal, Riverglades and Zephras Isle.

  10. Dec 9
    The first raid tier

    Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit (20 players, 13 bosses) and Onyxia’s Lair.

Questions veterans ask

Short answers, each pointing to the page with the long one.

The game

Is Forever just Vanilla again?

No. It keeps the old world, its pace and its rules: outdoor mobs still take 10–15 seconds to kill, and threat, crowd control, mana and positioning still matter. But classes, races and systems change wherever Vanilla was incomplete or awkward, and the story is free to go somewhere new.

The classes book

Is it Retail rewound, then?

Not that either. New abilities rarely turn a class into Wrath, Cataclysm or modern rotational WoW. Mostly they add a meaningful action every few seconds, repair a broken resource loop, or give a spec a real capstone.

How the classes changed

When does it launch, and when do raids open?

Launch is November 4, 2026. The first raid tier, Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair, is scheduled for December 9.

The patch log

What’s the level cap?

60. Blizzard describes Forever as a permanent level-60 branch.

The reveal, in the patch log

Is there dual spec?

Yes, from level 40. It’s one of the biggest lifestyle changes for Paladins, Priests, Druids and Shamans.

System changes

Does downranking still work?

Much less. Spells far below the caster’s level lose spell-power benefit and proc chances. Where the trick did something useful, a real spell replaces it, like the Mage’s Frost Blast.

System changes

Do buffs still have to be juggled across parties?

Far less. Battle Shout, Trueshot Aura, Moonkin Aura, Leader of the Pack and four Shaman totems are raid-wide, and Salvation no longer competes with other Blessings.

System changes

Classes

Which classes changed the most?

Paladin, Shaman and Druid, on the codex’s own reckoning. Hunter, Priest, Mage and Warlock gain distinct spec loops; Warrior and Rogue stay closest to their Classic originals.

Distance from Vanilla

Do Alliance Paladins finally have a taunt?

Yes. Protection’s Seal of Fury has a Judgment that taunts, and Consecration is baseline at level 20.

The Paladin sheet

Can the Alliance play Shaman?

Yes, as Dwarves only, and the class is heavily redesigned besides: one-second totem globals, five-minute buff totems and presets that drop a whole set.

The Shaman sheet

Is powershifting still a thing?

Formalized. Tiger’s Fury is gone, and Shifting Power converts base mana into Energy on a cooldown, so the old trick is now a talent.

The Druid sheet

Do Hunters still have a dead zone?

Yes. Survival is built to move through the ranges with useful actions on both sides, not to erase range management.

The Hunter sheet

Are the class numbers final?

No. The October 16 build was the last with class changes, and Blizzard has said talents and abilities can keep moving. Mechanical identity is a better guide than a beta damage ranking.

Choosing a path

Races

Is Human still the melee race?

No. Weapon-skill racials are gone for good: Human’s Sword and Mace Specialization became +1% crit with any weapon.

The Human sheet

Do raids still need Dwarf Priests for Fear Ward?

No. Every Priest learns Fear Ward at level 20, and Devouring Plague is baseline too.

The Dwarf sheet

Which race and class pairs are new?

Human Hunter, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman, plus the High Order Skyborne, who can be Warriors, Hunters, Rogues, Druids and Mages.

The five races

Can any account play a Skyborne?

No. Blizzard gates the Skyborne race, and the Zephras Isle starting questline, behind the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher.

The Skyborne sheet

Are Draenei or Worgen in?

Not at launch, and none of Retail’s later Alliance races. Blizzard has said more race and class combinations can come later, which isn’t the same as new playable races.

Not on the roster

Is Shadowmeld a second Vanish now?

No. In combat it works like Fade: threat and targeting relief, but no Stealth-required abilities.

Tooltip checks

Which race is best?

Too early to say. The final racial pass landed nine days before launch and no mature level-60 data exists; for most specs no race is wrong.

Race leans by class

Companions

Can the Collector’s Edition pets be had now?

Yes. Forever’s Skyborne Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection include the Zergling, Panda Cub and Mini Diablo, along with the new Pachimari. The Collection is advertised through January 11, 2027; the Epic Pack has no stated end.

Paid and subscription pets

Do companions carry over between characters?

Within the account, it seems: Wowhead’s Forever database flags many companions account-wide. Whether they cross factions isn’t confirmed, and Blizzard hasn’t published the rules.

What changed

Are the old pet drop rates the same?

Not all of them. On the beta, Cookie’s Siamese dropped about 9–10% of the time, against Classic’s 20%, while the Hyacinth Macaw stayed a one-in-several-thousand farm.

The odds, measured

Are there pet battles?

No. Forever’s companions are cosmetic and non-combat.

The companions book

The story

Does Forever follow The Burning Crusade and Wrath?

Not necessarily. Forever lives in its own time bubble: history up to early Year 1 is foundation, and the later expansions are precedent, not destiny. Blizzard hasn’t committed to replaying any of them.

Three things not to assume

Could Forever change how the battle at Hyjal went?

No. Archimonde’s defeat is already part of Forever’s history; the branch comes later.

The Third War

Who are the Skyborne?

Descendants of Highborne rebels who fled Eldre’Thalas after the War of the Ancients, now losing their wind patrons. The High Order side with the Alliance.
