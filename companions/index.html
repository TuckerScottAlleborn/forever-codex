The Companions

The Vanilla menagerie is still here. The collection built around it is new.

Vanilla’s companions were a short list of bag-space vanity items and a handful of brutal world drops. Forever keeps the old menagerie, from Donni Anthania’s cats to the Hyacinth Macaw, and builds a real collection around it: companions are learned and account-wide rather than carried, and new ones come from rares, quests, emotes, reputation, Legacy and a Black Market Auction House.

This book follows the Alliance side: the 64 companions within an Alliance character’s reach, with the twelve in Horde cities listed separately. Launch is November 4, so the drop counts here come from the beta, and rare respawns and the first Forever holidays can’t be confirmed yet; each uncertain detail is marked.

since vanilla

What changed

The pets a veteran remembers are mostly where they were. What surrounds them isn’t.

Learned, and account-wide

Classic companions were bag items, one per character. Forever’s teach a summon into the account’s Collections, which Blizzard says are account-wide across every ruleset, so one copy serves every character. Whether the other faction can summon a faction’s own pets isn’t spelled out. They stay cosmetic and non-combat: there are no pet battles.

Trading, and duplicates

Most returning pets are still bind on use, so they trade before they’re learned, and with account-wide unlocks a duplicate becomes a trade good rather than a copy for an alt. The new progression pets, such as the Spectral Bear Cub, Bitter Baitling and Minimule, bind on pickup.

The Collector’s Edition trio returns

The Zergling, Panda Cub and Mini Diablo were locked to the original 2004 Collector’s Edition. Forever’s Skyborne Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection include all three, with the new Pachimari. The Collection is advertised through January 11, 2027; the Epic Pack has no stated end.

New pets in the old world

Rares now carry pets in Loch Modan, Darkshore and Stormwind’s canals. A new quest gives Weevil K. Neevil, and the old vendors stock new colors, such as Brown and Arctic Rabbits and an Eagle Owl.

New systems that pay in pets

Legacy (the Spectral Bear Cub at 25 points), the Nightclaw Druids (Shadowgale Squirrel), Waylaid Crates and Merchant’s Favor (Minimule), the Fishing Extravaganza’s coin (Bitter Baitling), and a Black Market Auction House Vanilla never had, which sells two new whelplings.

Emotes as keys

/sing to frogs for the Musical Gustjumper and /dance at a sewer slime for Excitable Slime, beside the old /chicken and /beckon rituals for the Prairie and Ancona Chickens.

Some odds moved

Cookie’s Siamese dropped about 9–10% of the time on the beta, against Classic’s 20%, and Peddlefeet is now a rare gift drop, not a 40-token purchase. The Hyacinth Macaw is as long a farm as ever. The beta’s drop counts.

In Horde cities

Twelve more companions sit with Horde vendors and quests. Most are bind-on-use items that trade before they’re learned, and Collections are account-wide, so one learned on a Horde character joins the account’s collection. Whether an Alliance character can then summon it isn’t spelled out, so they aren’t counted here.

  • OrgrimmarXan’tish sells Black Kingsnake, Brown Snake, Crimson Snake and the new Jungle Boa (52s 63c); the roaming Mayhoa Skyhoof sells the new Prairie Wolf Pup (63s 15c).

an alliance route

Collecting in order

An efficient order for an Alliance collector who would rather not backtrack. It favors what can be planned over the rarest trophy.

  1. 1While leveling

    The ten Alliance vendor pets, among them two new rabbits and a new owl, and the Prairie Chicken in Westfall. Lord Condar in Loch Modan and Strider Clutchmother in Darkshore are worth a look while passing through: with no reliable respawn timer or drop rate yet, a passing check beats a dedicated trip back.

  2. 2An early collection pass

    Narkk’s two parrots in Booty Bay, the Ancona Chicken, Weevil K. Neevil, the White Kitten, and the OOX beacons for the Mechanical Chicken.

  3. 3Forever’s systems, from day one

    Waylaid Crates toward 1,500 Merchant’s Favor and Revered with the Azeroth Commerce Authority for the Minimule, and Legacy toward 25 points for the Spectral Bear Cub. Neither ends in a drop chance, and starting late only means backtracking.

  4. 4Zephras Isle, where the account has access

    The Musical Gustjumper is nearly free once there. The Nightclaw Druids grind to Revered for the Shadowgale Squirrel is the long project.

  5. 5The weekly calendar

    Sunday’s Fishing Extravaganza, where only the first 50 Master Angler turn-ins earn the coin for Bitter Baitling, and the Darkmoon Faire for its two frogs and Jubling.

  6. 6Stormwind’s canals

    Sewer Beast for the Baby Crocolisk: a level-50 elite inside the Alliance capital, likely to be contested around launch.

  7. 7The market

    The Black Market Auction House at Powderfuse Port, for the new Tiny Azure and Tiny Bronze Whelplings and Pet Fish, and as a fallback for the Siamese, both macaws and the Black Tabby.

  8. 8The long tail

    The three whelplings, the Disgusting Oozeling, the Hyacinth Macaw and the Engineering specialty schematics.

  9. 9The holidays, as they arrive

    Winter Veil, Love is in the Air, the Lunar Festival, Children’s Week, Midsummer and Hallow’s End.

easiest to miss

The brutal world drops never go away. The weekly and account-gated pets are the ones a collector can miss: Bitter Baitling’s weekly race, Zephras access, Legacy progress, the Engineering specialty pets, the Warcraft Forever Collection’s window, advertised through January 11, 2027 (the same pets also come with the Skyborne Epic Pack), and Azurky’s offer, which must be redeemed by April 30, 2027.
