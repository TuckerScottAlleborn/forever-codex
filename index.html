Forever Codex

The old world on a new branch: its story is precedent, not destiny, and its classes are Classic, finished.

Forever isn’t Vanilla again, and it isn’t retail rewound. It keeps the world and the history Warcraft veterans know, then lets both move. This codex reads it in three books: the lore, on what’s canon and what’s still unwritten; the classes, on how the nine Alliance classes play against Vanilla; and the races, on what each Alliance race is for now that weapon skill is gone.
